HOW PROJECTS WORK

Build with a developer mindset.

The goal is not simply to finish a project. It is to understand how and why the solution works.

01

Think

Understand the problem, requirements and expected outcome before writing code.

02

Plan

Break the solution into manageable technical components and development tasks.

03

Build

Implement the solution while applying the concepts learned during the process.

04

Improve

Test, review and refine the project to develop stronger problem-solving skills.
